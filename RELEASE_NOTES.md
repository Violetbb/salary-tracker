# Salary Tracker v0.1.0

## Download

Download `SalaryTracker.dmg`, open it, and drag Salary Tracker into Applications.

## Compatibility

- macOS 13 or newer
- Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (Universal binary)

## First launch

This initial build is ad hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. In Finder, Control-click Salary Tracker in Applications, choose Open, then confirm Open in the dialog. Future launches should work normally.

Salary and leave data are stored locally on your Mac. The app does not send them to a server.
